## HoldOption

```go
type HoldOption func(*asked)
```

HoldOption is how a hold asks for its key.

### InBackground

```go
func InBackground() HoldOption
```

InBackground asks for a semaphore's place in the background: behind every urgent hold of the key, and among no more than the semaphore's Background places. Nothing promises it a place: while urgent holds keep coming and take the room, it waits, its Wait at most.

### Timeout

```go
func Timeout(d time.Duration) HoldOption
```

Timeout is how long a semaphore's hold may keep its place, from its grant: past it the place goes to the next in line, fn's ctx ends and the hold is ErrLost, as at MaxHold, the shorter of the two counting.

### Wait

```go
func Wait(d time.Duration) HoldOption
```

Wait is how long a hold waits in line for its key, 10 s unless given: a key still held past it is ErrInUse.

## Lease

```go
type Lease struct {
	Fence  Fence
	Waited time.Duration
}
```

Lease is a holding of a key: a lock's Fence, none for a semaphore's, and how long the hold Waited for it, from its call to the grant, the round trip included.

### Lock.Hold

```go
func (l *Lock) Hold(ctx context.Context, key any, fn func(ctx context.Context, lease *Lease) error, opts ...HoldOption) error
```

Hold waits for key in line, holds it while fn runs and lets it go. A key still held past the wait is ErrInUse; a lease lost while fn ran ends its ctx, and Hold is ErrLost though fn succeeded, since another holder may have run beside it.

## Option

```go
type Option func(*keys)
```

Option is how a lock or a semaphore opens.

### Background

```go
func Background(n int) Option
```

Background is how many of a semaphore's key's places background holds may take at once, 1 to its Limit, so that urgent holds always find the rest; every place unless given.

### Limit

```go
func Limit(n int) Option
```

Limit is how many may hold a semaphore's key at once, 1 to 1000.

### MaxHold

```go
func MaxHold(d time.Duration) Option
```

MaxHold is how long a lease lasts from its grant, however it is renewed: past it the next in line holds the key, and the holder's ctx ends.

### TTL

```go
func TTL(d time.Duration) Option
```

TTL is how long a lease lasts from its last renewal, 1 s to 1 hour, 10 s unless given; this program renews it at a third of it while the function runs.

### Semaphore.Hold

```go

```

Hold waits for one of key's places in line and holds it while fn runs, as a lock's Hold does; InBackground and Timeout say in which line it waits and how long it may keep its place:
